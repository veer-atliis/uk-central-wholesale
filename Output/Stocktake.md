# Complete your stocktake

1. Open the attached Excel template.
2. Count each product and variant separately.
3. Enter the total in **Counted Quantity**, using the **Unit of Measure** shown. Convert cartons or packs to this unit before entering the count.
4. Enter **0** if none are found. Leave blank only if you have not counted the item.
5. Add any issues in **Notes**. Keep the product details and **Product System ID** unchanged.
6. Return the completed file with your name and the count date and time. Mention any unlisted stock or stock movements during the count.

**Attachment:** UK Central Wholesale - Stocktake Template.xlsx
